Account & Cloud Data Deletion Portal

In compliance with Google Play User Data & Account Deletion Policies, users of Overtime Tracker BD have the complete right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated cloud-stored records.

⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once processed, your cloud backup will be wiped, and your past overtime or salary calculations cannot be restored.

1. Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon receiving and verifying your request, the following records stored on our Google Firebase Firestore backend will be permanently purged:

  • User Profile: Name, Company Name, Worker ID, Designation, Grade, and Settings.
  • Salary Structure: Gross Salary, Basic Salary, Allowances, Attendance Bonus, and Custom Rates.
  • Overtime & Shift Logs: All daily overtime hours, notes, and shift entries.
  • Piece Rate Records: All piece-rate products, counts, and amounts.
  • Authentication Identifier: Google Sign-In UID link associated with your account.

2. Processing Timeline & Retention

After verification, your account and associated cloud data will be permanently deleted within 7 business days, subject to applicable legal requirements. No residual personal or financial data is retained on our cloud servers. If you are also using the app locally on your device, simply click "Sign Out" in the app's Profile screen to instantly wipe locally cached records.
